Frequently Asked Questions

Documentation questions

How do I get a spec sheet for a specific Florestone product?

All current spec sheets are available for download on this page in PDF format. If you need a spec sheet for a discontinued model or require a project-specific document, contact our sales team at (800) 446-2647 or email info@florestone.com.

What CAD file formats are available for Florestone products?

Florestone provides section CAD drawings in DWG and PDF formats for all current series. Files include plan view, elevation, and section views suitable for architectural and engineering submissions.

Can I request custom documentation for a large project submittal?

Yes. For healthcare, multifamily, hospitality, or institutional projects requiring custom engineer-submittal packages — including custom CAD, ADA documentation, and IAPMO certification letters — contact our sales team at (800) 446-2647.

Need a custom spec package?

For project submittals, volume orders, and custom documentation — our sales team will pull exactly what your spec engineer needs.
