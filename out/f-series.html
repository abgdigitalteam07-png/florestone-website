F Series
RTM Fiberglass w/ AcrylX™

F Series is Florestone's RTM fiberglass platform. Resin Transfer Molded shells are laminated with our proprietary AcrylX™ applied acrylic and a composite reinforcement matrix — delivering a tough, repairable, color-matched surface in shower bases, one-piece stalls, tub-shower units (60×32/60×34/60×36/60×42) and three-piece wall systems for remodel work. The line where Florestone's 60+ years of plastics manufacturing meets modern installation realities.

Why the F Series

Engineered for the work.

01

AcrylX™ Surface

Florestone's proprietary applied acrylic — color-through, stain-resistant, repairable in the field. Lifetime warranted in residential use, 30 years commercial.

02

Compression-Molded Heritage

Florestone was one of the first manufacturers of compression-molded shower receptors when we pioneered the process in 1965. F Series is that legacy in modern materials.

03

Full Bath Configurations

Beyond bases: one-piece shower stalls, 3-piece walls, and 60-inch tub-shower combos (6032TS, 6034TS, 6036TS, 6042TS) for full remodels and rentals.

04

The Edge Treatment

Optional Edge profile — Florestone's premium tile-transition detail at the perimeter. A featured Florestone innovation on RTM and recess bases.

05

Built for the Trade

Lightweight against terrazzo, faster install, and finished color-through — what plumbers reach for on volume builds and renovation specs.

06

Two US Plants

Produced in Madera, California and Denison, Texas — duo-coast manufacturing for shorter lead times to your jobsite.
